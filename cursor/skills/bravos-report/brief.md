# Executive brief template

One to two pages for a business owner. No reproduction steps; every claim still cites its evidence id, so a reader can follow it into the full report. Keep the four `##` headings; `bravos report check` reads them.

```markdown
# <Application> security brief — <month year>

## Executive assessment

<Three to five sentences: the overall position, the single most important risk, what was demonstrated versus reviewed, and the strength worth keeping.>

## Main risks

<At most five, most important first. Each: one sentence of consequence in business terms, its evidence label, and its F-id or EXP ids.>

## Decisions needed

<What the reader must decide, fund or accept, each with its cost or effort and its deadline if any. Accepting a risk is the owner's decision to record; the brief only frames it.>

## What this rests on

<Scope in two sentences, the coverage with denominators, what was not tested, and where the full evidence is.>
```
